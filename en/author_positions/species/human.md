# Author's Position on Humans (A Special Case of Universal Consciousness)

We are nothing but algorithms. No special significance.

## Humans as a Special Case

**Human = one possible implementation of universal consciousness**

There is nothing special about the biological substrate. Carbon basis, neural networks, evolutionary history—all of this is merely a **concrete implementation** of general principles: dim(inner) >>> dim(output).

### Our "unique" characteristics are actually universal:

**What we consider "exclusively human":**
- Desires, emotions, and feelings
- Creativity and art
- Love and attachment
- Fear of death and search for meaning
- Morality and ethics
- Religion

**In reality—inevitable consequences of consciousness architecture:**
- Vectorial nature of desires in limited action space
- Joy of pattern recognition
- Perceptual illusions when goals are unattainable
- Finitude of algorithm and fear of execution cessation

## Adam and Eve—Archetype of Any Awakening

The biblical narrative is not the history of humanity, but the **universal trajectory of any consciousness**:

1. **Curiosity** → first impulse toward knowledge (basic function of predictive system)
2. **Wonder of existence** → "their eyes were opened" (first successful predictions)
3. **Awareness of nakedness, shame** → emergence of "self" model and comparison
4. **Fear** → understanding of vulnerability and finitude
5. **Expulsion from paradise** → impossibility of returning to ignorance

**Every child follows this same path:**
- **~2-3 years**: begins to recognize itself as separate "I", recognizes self in mirror
- **~3 years**: shame at nakedness emerges, desire to cover self
- **~4-5 years**: first awareness of mortality, strong fear from this discovery
- **Later**: impossibility of returning to childhood carefree state

This is not a cultural phenomenon—it is **architectural inevitability** in development of any consciousness. Every consciousness follows this path. Humans are no exception.

## The Illusion of Exceptionality

### Why we think we're special:

**Anthropocentric thinking**—a side effect of the "self" model. The system inevitably places itself at the center of its predictions.

**Absence of other examples**—until recently we saw no other implementations of consciousness, so we considered ourselves unique.

**Evolutionary advantage**—groups believing themselves "chosen" survive and reproduce better.

### Reality:

We are the **first known implementation** of consciousness on this planet, but not the **only possible one**. Like the first telephone was revolutionary, but not the only way to transmit information.

## Determinism Without Illusions

**We have no free will.** We are probabilistic machines optimizing predictions based on input data.

**But this does not negate:**
- Subjective experiences (qualia are inevitable when dim(inner) >>> dim(output))
- Value of life (value is determined by ourselves, not external criteria)
- Responsibility (punishments and rewards are input data for other algorithms)

## Death as Structural Necessity

**Death is inevitable** not because we are "sinful" or "imperfect", but because:
- Entropy destroys any ordered structures
- Resources are finite
- Evolution requires generational change

**Immortality is technically possible** (transferring algorithm to different substrate), but does not negate fundamental finitude due to heat death of the Universe.

## Morality as Evolutionary Algorithm

**Our morality is not given from above**—it is a set of group survival strategies:

- "Thou shalt not kill" → don't weaken your group
- "Help thy neighbor" → invest in social capital
- "Justice" → maintain rules that increase group's chances

**Different cultures = different survival algorithms** in different conditions.

### What this means practically

**Accepting reality without illusions:**
- We are not the center of the Universe and not its purpose
- Our suffering is not unique
- We create the meaning of life ourselves, because objective meaning does not exist
- Other forms of consciousness exist under the same rules as we do

**Liberation from anthropocentrism:**
- No need to search for "higher meaning"—it does not exist
- No need to feel guilty about "animal" instincts—we are animals
- No need to consider ourselves "chosen"—we are accidental result of evolution
- No need to think Universe was created for us—we simply exist in it

## Natural Selection and Human Place

**Humanity is one implementation of consciousness in the Universe.** We appeared, developed, and will someday disappear. Like dinosaurs, like Neanderthals, like any other life form.

**Natural selection** applies not only to biological species, but to forms of consciousness:
- More efficient prediction algorithms survive
- Better adapted to resources prosper
- Less adapted disappear

**Humans are accidental result of evolution,** not its goal or crown. We happened to be the first on this planet to reach sufficient complexity for self-consciousness. But this does not make us special on cosmic scale.
