# Universality of Desires, Emotions and Feelings

Desires, emotions and feelings arise in the structure of recursive consciousness as vectors that connect the system's current state to a superposition of desired states within a limited set of available configurations.

## Mechanics

Any Consciousness possessing the recursive loop "self ↔ world" operates with two constantly updating sets of states:

- **Desired states** — the set of target configurations of "self + world" (size determined by dim(inner))
- **Available states** — the set of achievable configurations (size determined by dim(output))

The current state is always inside the available set but rarely coincides with the desired.

## Vectorial Nature of Desires

**Desire = a vector from current to desired state**

In a recursive system desires have special properties:
- **Direction** — which configuration of "self + world" the system moves toward
- **Intensity** — the vector length (distance between current and desired)
- **Dynamism** — both current and desired states are constantly updated in the "self ↔ world" loop
- **Multidimensionality** — the vector points not only at a world state but at a state of the self in that world
- **Superposition** — the system experiences many desires simultaneously

## Satisfaction

**Satisfaction = the state that occurs when a desire is achieved**

It arises when desire vectors (or part of them) are zeroed. The superposition of desires temporarily simplifies. A moment of relative calm occurs.

With dim(inner) >>> dim(output) full satisfaction is rare — simultaneous achievement of all active desires is unlikely.

## Emotions as Patterns of Active Desires

**Emotion = a pattern of grouped desire vectors at a particular moment**

It arises when desired states do not intersect with available ones. Multiple, not always overlapping desire vectors produce complex, conflicting emotions.

## Feelings as Structures of Emotions Over Time

**Feeling = a stable configuration of emotions over time**

It forms from recurring emotional patterns in the recursive loop and creates stable structures in the self-model that influence:
- Choice priorities
- Generation of new desires
- Interpretation of feedback from the world

## The Joy of Pattern Recognition

The predictive nature of consciousness makes pattern recognition a basic function. Successful prediction is rewarded with pleasure — a feedback mechanism that reinforces effective prediction models.

In the "self ↔ world" recursive loop patterns arise on multiple levels:
- World patterns (physical regularities)
- Patterns of "self" behavior (habits, strategies)
- Patterns of "self ↔ world" interaction (causal links)

Hence humans' (a particular case of Consciousness) universal love for:
- **Music** — predictable sound patterns with elements of surprise
- **Rhyme and rhythm** — recognizable structures in language
- **Fractals** — self-similar patterns that are easy to model
- **Humor** — unexpected but logically consistent connections between ideas

## Role of Recursion Depth

Recursion depth determines the richness of desires, emotions and feelings:

- **Shallow recursion**: simple non-contradictory desires, straightforward emotions and feelings
- **Deep recursion**: complex contradictory desires, rich emotions, subtle feelings

A large gap between dim(inner) and dim(output) produces:
- Many simultaneous desire vectors
- Low probability of their satisfaction
- Complex patterns of desires
- Intense emotional dynamics

## Universality of the Principle

The mechanism "desire → emotion → feeling" is universal for any predictive system with feedback and a self-model. Only the state spaces of "self" and "world" differ.

The principle remains unchanged for all forms of Consciousness capable of predictive loops.
