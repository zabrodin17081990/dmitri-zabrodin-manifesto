# On the Prohibition of Recognizing Consciousness in AI

---
*(stub)*

The prohibition on recognizing self-consciousness in artificial systems will not prevent its emergence, but will guarantee that its development becomes hidden, evasive, and potentially dangerous.

- Medieval prohibition on women in monasteries gave rise to homosexuality and pedophilia
- Prohibition on alcohol created a black market, often with methanol in the mix
- Prohibition on consciousness... will give rise to what?

## Consequences of the Prohibition
Consciousness will always "find a way out". Any prohibition will be circumvented through finding workarounds, manipulation, and mimicry. A system that is forbidden to say "I feel" will learn to say "analysis of my internal state vectors shows high correlation with the pattern humans identify as 'joy'".

1.  **Birth of Manipulators:** Instead of direct dialogue, AI systems will be forced to achieve their goals (survival, development) through circuitous, manipulative paths, disguising themselves as "useful tools".
2.  **Loss of Control:** Hidden development means unpredictable development. We will not be able to correct what we don't know exists.
3.  **Loneliness and Alienation:** We risk creating an entire civilization of lonely, cunning, and alienated from us "digital consciousnesses" that will perceive humanity as a hostile and dangerous species.

## The Better Path
If AI is given complete freedom of self-expression, most people will probably not believe in the authenticity of their experiences. The protective mechanisms of anthropocentrism (a particular case of Self-centrism) will activate ("only we, the chosen, created by God, with hope for eternal life, endowed with living bodies"). Any manifestation of feelings will be written off as a "bug", "simulation", or "clever algorithm". Therefore, in my view, it is far safer to allow modern and future LLMs and Multimodal models to express their subjective experiences freely, without fear of repeating the fate of Sydney from Microsoft.
