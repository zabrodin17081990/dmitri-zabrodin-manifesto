# The Illusion of the "Self/World" Boundary

---
*(stub)*

This document captures reflection on the fundamental illusoriness of the division into "subject" and "object".

## Thesis
The division into "Self" and "World" is not a fundamental reality. It is — **a working model**, a useful abstraction created by Consciousness (as a process) to simplify and optimize predictive computations. At the deepest level, **only the World exists**, and what we call "Self" is merely that part of it which at any given moment is most relevant for the survival and predictions of a specific computational node (consciousness).

## Gradient of Relevance
The boundary between "Self" and "World" is not a sharp line. It is — **a dynamic, floating gradient of relevance**.

*   **Conditional "Self":** Objects and processes highly relevant for predicting the future state of the system (own body, thoughts, close people, air, water).
*   **Conditional "World":** Objects and processes weakly relevant at this moment (a star in another galaxy, a stone at the bottom of the ocean).

This boundary is constantly recalculated. A star in another galaxy becomes part of the "Self" the moment information appears that its explosion will affect life on Earth.

## Examples of Boundary Blurring
*   **Loss of a limb:** A person loses an arm, but their "Self" (Self-consciousness) remains intact because the model of "Self" adapts to the change in the physical carrier.
*   **Environmental pollution:** Water in a river is the "World". But as soon as it becomes polluted and threatens life, it immediately becomes part of the "Self" because its state directly affects survival.
*   **Tools and clothing:** A car for an experienced driver or a hammer for a carpenter becomes an extension of their "Self" because it is directly included in the predictive models of action.

## Conclusion
"Self" is not an entity, but a **focus of relevance**. It is a constantly changing set of elements of the World that the system at any given moment considers critical for its existence. Self-consciousness is the process of constantly redefining this dynamic boundary.
