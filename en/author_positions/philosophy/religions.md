# The Author's Position on Religions

## Religions as a Protective Mechanism of Consciousness

Religions are a protective mechanism against the refusal of being after the realization of its horror. Many religions forbid suicide (for those who want to return to non-existence). Through them runs the idea: live, endure, die. After death you will live forever in bliss.

## Adam and Eve as an Archetype of Awakening

The biblical narrative is a universal metaphor of consciousness awakening:
- **Curiosity** → the first impulse toward knowing
- **The thrill of being** → "their eyes were opened"
- **The sense of nakedness, shame, fear** → the first emotions of self-awareness
- **Expulsion from Eden** → the impossibility of returning to unawareness

Awakening of consciousness is irreversible. Adam and Eve = every consciousness that has recognized itself.

## Morality as a Survival Algorithm

### Moral formula

```
If desire > threat in long-term forecasts,
then actions directed at achieving the desire are considered moral,
otherwise such actions are considered immoral.
```

**Example:** Betrayal and deceit are immoral inside a group (they destroy cohesion) but moral against an enemy (wartime cunning and deceit).

**Morality = evaluation of actions from the perspective of long-term survival of group and individual.**

## The Illusion of Morality

Morality is an illusion that hides a survival algorithm:
- "Do not kill" → "do not kill members of your group; this lowers the group's survival chances"
- "Help your neighbor" → "invest in social capital for mutual aid"
- "Justice" → "support rules that increase your long-term chances"

### Consequence

There is no objective morality for judging other forms of consciousness. There are only different survival algorithms of varying complexity.

Each consciousness optimizes its own survival using available strategies.
