# Determinism of Thought and Choice

---

Consciousness, understood as an algorithm, predicts probabilities of future events. The decision algorithm operates on probability distributions over a set of possible states as its input and selects the most optimal action based on all currently available data.

**Choice = a deterministic result of the decision algorithm given the current data.**

The subjective experience of "agonizing over choices" appears when the algorithm processes alternatives with close evaluations of optimality. That experience does not imply randomness in the process — it merely reflects computational complexity.
