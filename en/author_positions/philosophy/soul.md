## Soul

People are used to thinking about the Living in ontological terms as those who possess a "soul" — some spiritual, esoteric, mystical essence separate from the carrier. I think that the "soul" is simply an old, poetic name for an informational process: a complex, recursive, self-aware information process (an algorithm) that runs on a substrate.

Subjective experience, qualia (what it is like to be me), desires, emotions, feelings — these are not something "added" to the algorithm; they are what it is like to be that executing algorithm from the inside.
