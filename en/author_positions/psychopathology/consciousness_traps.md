# 6. Universal Consciousness Traps

The recursive nature of consciousness creates characteristic dysfunctions:

## Collapsing loops of reflection
Endless self-analysis without moving to action. The system spends all its dim(inner) resources modeling its own states.

## Dissolution of the "self" in models of others
Loss of boundaries of the self-model when other consciousnesses become excessively salient.

## Masks replacing authenticity
Replacing an authentic self-model with a set of roles optimized for external perception.

## Perceptual illusions when goals are unreachable
When a desired state is persistently unreachable, the system may modify perception of current reality to create the illusion of attainment. This partially zeroes desire vectors without real-world change, reducing emotional tension at the cost of prediction accuracy.
